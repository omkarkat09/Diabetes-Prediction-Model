About This Model

Machine Learning Algorithm

This predictive dashboard utilizes a Random Forest Classifier trained on the well-known Pima Indians Diabetes Database. The model evaluates eight distinct health parameters to estimate the probability of diabetes onset.

Feature Importance

Beyond providing a single risk score, the model uses feature importance analysis to explain why a certain score was given. It highlights which specific medical parameters are driving your risk up or down.

Medical Disclaimer

Educational purposes only. This application is intended as a demonstration of machine learning capabilities and should never replace professional medical diagnosis, advice, or treatment.
